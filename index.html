Welcome Message

A Message from Our Principal

Welcome to Eaglenest Academy, where we are committed to nurturing well-rounded learners who embody the values enshrined in Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum.

Our approach goes beyond academic excellence. We focus on developing critical thinking, creativity, communication, collaboration, and strong character in every child. We believe that education should prepare learners not just for exams, but for life.

At Eaglenest, every child matters. Our dedicated team of educators works tirelessly to create a safe, inclusive, and stimulating environment where your child can discover their unique talents and reach their full potential.

We invite you to join our community of excellence.

Mrs. Grace Mwangi
Principal, Eaglenest Academy

Our Difference

Why Choose Eaglenest Academy?

We provide a holistic learning experience that prepares your child for success in academics, character, and life skills.

CBC Excellence

Fully aligned with Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum, we develop core competencies and values in every learner through innovative teaching methods.

Qualified Teachers

Our team comprises TSC-registered, experienced educators who are passionate about nurturing talent and committed to continuous professional development.

Modern Facilities

State-of-the-art classrooms, well-equipped science and computer labs, expansive playgrounds, and a comprehensive library support holistic learning.

Small Class Sizes

We maintain optimal teacher-to-learner ratios (1:15) ensuring personalized attention, meaningful interaction, and tailored support for every child.

Safe & Secure

With 24/7 security, CCTV monitoring, secure perimeter fencing, and vetted staff, we provide parents with complete peace of mind.

Co-Curricular Activities

Diverse clubs, sports, music, drama, and STEM activities help learners discover their passions and develop talents beyond the classroom.
